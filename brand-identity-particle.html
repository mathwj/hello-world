01Brand Story

Every voice is a particle — small on its own, but together they form constellations.

Aloud exists to amplify the unheard. We believe that every thought spoken aloud creates a ripple — a particle of energy that connects, resonates, and builds something greater than itself.

Our identity draws from the physics of particles: individual points of light drifting through space, forming bonds, creating patterns. Like voices in conversation, particles alone are quiet. Together, they illuminate.

17Motion Principles

Four core animation principles that govern all movement in the Aloud experience.

Float

Gentle, ambient drift. Particles move slowly and never stop.

Connect

Lines draw between points to show relationships forming.

Pulse

Glow expands and contracts like breathing. Shows life and energy.

Scatter

Particles burst outward from a point. Used for emphasis moments.

20Do’s & Don’ts

Quick reference for maintaining brand consistency across all touchpoints.

Do

Use the particle system on dark backgrounds. The particles need contrast to glow.

Do

Maintain generous whitespace around the logo. Let the particles breathe.

Do

Use Outfit for headlines and Inter for body text. Keep the type system consistent.

Do

Apply the grain texture overlay on all brand surfaces for tactile depth.

Don’t

Never place particles on light or white backgrounds. They lose all impact.

Don’t

Never use more than 8-10 particles in a single composition. Less is more.

Don’t

Never substitute brand colors with similar shades. Use exact hex values only.

Don’t

Never make particle animations fast or jarring. Motion should always be calm and ambient.
